**Version 1.0 · 27 October 2026.** This manual describes the Sbarter app as a player experiences it, screen by screen, and shows how the app and an integrated game work together across the life of a contest. It is written for Video Game Providers – management, product, marketing, support and engineering teams – who need to understand what their players will see and do once their title is live on Sbarter. It is not a technical reference: the API contracts are in the Integration Guides and the API Reference of this portal.
